What we do

Programs built for curious classrooms.

We design every Clinically session to fit into a single class period — 45 to 60 minutes of focused, honest, and interactive conversation about what it really takes to enter medicine. Every program happens in person, in Michigan schools.

01

In-School Presentations

Our flagship program. A trained Clinically presenter comes to your school and delivers a tailored, interactive talk that demystifies the premed pathway — from high school course selection to med school applications.

  • 45–60 min
  • Grades 9–12
  • Free for schools
02

Curated Course Tracks

We build short, focused courses for students aiming at colleges and universities with strong medical pathways — which high school classes actually matter, how premed, nursing, and BS/MD programs differ, what each application really asks for, and how to build a profile that gets in.

  • Admissions-focused
  • College & program lists
  • Built by current premeds
03

Resource Library

A growing, free library of guides, timelines, and printable planning tools — covering everything from high school course selection to Michigan summer programs, shadowing etiquette, and MCAT prep timelines.

  • Always free
  • Printed + PDF
  • Updated yearly
04

Med Student Panels

On-site panels featuring current medical students from Michigan schools and a range of specialties. Students submit questions in advance and hear honest, lived-experience answers from people who just walked the path.

  • In person
  • By request
  • Q&A format
Why it matters

The premed path is opaque. We're making it readable.

Most high school students interested in medicine have no access to premed advising. The result? Talented students — especially those from underrepresented backgrounds — walk away from the path before they even start.

No Access

Most high schools offer no premed or health-career advising. Students are left to figure out a complex, high-stakes path entirely on their own — or not at all.

No Mentorship

Without guidance, talented students — especially those who are first-generation — often abandon the pathway before they even start college, due to lack of information, not lack of ability.

No Roadmap

The medical school pathway is opaque. Requirements, timelines, and exams can feel impossible to navigate without a clear, step-by-step map laid out by someone who has done it.

How it works

Booking is simple. Four steps.

From first email to presentation day, the process takes about two weeks. We handle content, materials, and presenter coordination — you just provide the classroom.

01

Reach Out

Submit the booking form below with your school, audience, and preferred dates. Takes less than three minutes.

02

Tailor Together

We schedule a 15-minute call to understand your students' needs and pick the right focus and presenter.

03

We Present

Our presenter drives out to your school and delivers the session in your classroom — interactive, real, and on time.

04

Follow-Up

Students leave with a printed roadmap, access to our resource library, and an invite to our curated course tracks.

Where we go

Michigan only. In person, always.

Clinically is based in Metro Detroit, and every session is delivered face-to-face in your building. We don't run virtual or online presentations — the conversations that change a student's mind happen in the room.
